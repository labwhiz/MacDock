What is RocketDock?

RocketDock is a free macOS-style dock launcher for Windows, developed by Punk Labs. It transforms your desktop by adding a smooth, animated dock — similar to the macOS Dock — compatible with Windows 7, 8, 10 and 11.

With RocketDock you can launch your favorite apps with one click, drag and drop icons directly into the dock, and customize its look with thousands of community-made skins. The hover zoom effect dynamically magnifies icons as your cursor approaches — just like macOS.

It also supports plugins called docklets that add extra functionality: clocks, recycle bin, volume control, and CPU monitor. At just 6.2 MB and written in optimized C++, it runs quietly in the background without slowing down your PC. A portable version is also available — no installation needed, runs straight from a USB drive.
